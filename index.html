Tournament Rules & Format

University Break Padel Championship (2 Courts)

Match Format & Timing

Each match is played as a best of 3 games (mini-sets). The first team to win 2 games wins the match. Designed to comfortably fit within your 1-hour university break (approx. 15-25 minutes per match including warm-up).

Round Robin Structure

Teams are divided into 3 groups of 6 teams each. Every team plays each other in their group once (5 matches per team). Matches are scheduled Sunday through Wednesday.
